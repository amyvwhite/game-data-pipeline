How accurate is it? (last 7 days)

Error = average % miss per hour (MAPE), lower is better. Seasonal = learns each game's daily rhythm. Baseline = "same hour yesterday". Ensemble = the average of the two. Skill above 0 means the champion beats the baseline.

    GTA VI launch watch (launches Nov 19 on PS5 / Xbox)

    GTA VI isn't on Steam, so its attention is measured on Twitch. Alongside it: each Take-Two game's Steam players vs its own average in the week before launch. Correlation, not proof.

    Do sales bring players back?

    Each sale compared with the week before it, minus the change in tracked games that were not on sale over the same days (a control group). That removes weekend, holiday and Steam-wide effects. Still observational: a sale that lands with a big update will look bigger.
